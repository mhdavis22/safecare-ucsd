Your privacy matters

SafeCare UCSD

Many college students rely on a parent’s health insurance. That can make privacy concerns feel like a barrier to getting sexual, reproductive, mental, or gender-affirming care.

You deserve to make informed choices about your health without worrying that sensitive care will be disclosed at home.

Use this guide to understand your options, protect your communications, and find confidential support. California law protects your right to confidential healthcare.

Why privacy matters

1. Select Your Health Insurance Plan

Key Fact for UC SHIP

With UC SHIP, you are the student receiving coverage and the primary policyholder for your student plan. Your parents may help pay for the plan, but paying for insurance does not make them the person whose EOBs should be sent to them.

Step 1

Create your Anthem account

Find your UC SHIP member ID in MyChart under Insurance. Use that member ID to create or activate your Anthem account at anthem.com/ca (or in the Sydney Health app).

Step 2

Set EOBs to email only

In your Anthem account, open Profile → Communication Preferences, then choose email or digital-only delivery for EOBs. This avoids paper mail when the setting is available.

  • Navigate to Profile → Communication Preferences.
  • Check that the email address belongs to you and that the change was saved.
Step 3

If you want paper mail sent somewhere else

Anthem pulls the mailing address for this account from TritonLink. If you want paper EOBs sent to your San Diego address instead of your parents’ home, change your primary address in TritonLink → Personal Tools → Current Address. This is the address source Anthem uses for UC SHIP.

California Law Protection (SB 138 / CHIA)

If you are covered as a dependent on a parent’s plan, your parent may be the primary policyholder (the person who owns the plan), while you are the patient receiving care. Under California law, health plans must honor a Confidential Communications Request (CCR) for sensitive care and direct those communications to you instead of the primary policyholder.[3]

Step 1

Locate Your Insurance Card

Find the customer-service phone number on the back of your insurance card. Have your insurance account or member number ready.

Step 2

Submit a Confidential Communication Request

Call your insurer and ask for a CCR form. Tell them where you want EOBs and other sensitive communications sent. UC SHIP students should use their Anthem account and TritonLink steps above instead of this script.

2. Confidential Communication Request Script

This script is for people on a parent’s plan who need to request a CCR. It is not needed for UC SHIP if you follow the Anthem steps above.

"Hi, my insurance account number is [YOUR_ACCOUNT_NUMBER]. I am going to receive sensitive healthcare and I need a Confidential Communication Request form. Please send my EOBs and related communications to me at [YOUR_EMAIL_OR_MAILING_ADDRESS] instead of to the primary policyholder. I am the person receiving the care."
