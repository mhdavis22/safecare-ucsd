Your privacy matters

SafeCare UCSD

Many college students rely on a parent’s health insurance. That can make privacy concerns feel like a barrier to getting sexual, reproductive, mental, or gender-affirming care.

You deserve to make informed choices about your health without worrying that sensitive care will be disclosed at home.

Use this guide to understand your options, protect your communications, and find confidential support. California law protects your right to confidential healthcare.

Why privacy matters

1. Select Your Health Insurance Plan

Key Fact for UC SHIP

As an adult UCSD student, you are the primary policyholder on UC SHIP—not your parents (even if they pay tuition). However, paper Explanations of Benefits (EOBs) can still be sent to your permanent home address unless updated.

Step 1

Update Address in TritonLink

Log into TritonLink → Personal Tools → Current Address. Change your primary address to your San Diego residence or campus mailbox so mail is not delivered home.

Step 2

Switch to Paperless EOBs (Anthem)

Log into anthem.com/ca or the Sydney Health App:

  • Navigate to Profile → Communication Preferences.
  • Under Plan Information / EOBs, select Email / Digital Only.
Step 3

Confirm Pharmacy Privacy (OptumRx)

When filling prescriptions at SHS Pharmacy or off-campus, explicitly request paperless receipts and list your personal mobile number for automated SMS notifications.

California Law Protection (SB 138 / CHIA)

Under California law, health plans MUST honor a Confidential Communications Request (CCR). Your insurer is legally prohibited from sharing sensitive care details (gender affirmation, reproductive health, STI care, mental health) with the primary policyholder.

Step 1

Locate Your Insurance Card

Find the Customer Service phone number on the back of your insurance card along with your Member ID.

Step 2

Submit a Confidential Communication Request

Call customer support or submit a written California CCR form to specify that all EOBs, claim details, and mail must be delivered to your private email or local San Diego address.

2. Confidential Communication Request Script

Use this precise language when calling your health insurance carrier to enforce California SB 138 confidentiality protections.

"Hello, I am a covered dependent on policy ID [YOUR_MEMBER_ID]. I would like to submit a Confidential Communication Request. I require all Explanations of Benefits (EOBs) and communications regarding sensitive health services to be sent exclusively to my personal email [YOUR_EMAIL] as the primary policyholder."
